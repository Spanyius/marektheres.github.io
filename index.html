How to book your room at Scandic
  1. Reserve via scandichotels.com or call the central reservations office on +46 8 517 517 00 and press 1.
  2. When booking, give the code BMAR101226 to access the pre-booked wedding rate.
  3. Rooms are booked subject to availability.
  4. Payment is made at check-in by credit card.
  5. Credit card information is required at the time of booking to guarantee the room.
  6. Bookings are made individually and are treated as individual reservations.
  7. A reservation can be changed or cancelled until 18:00 on the day before the stay.
  8. For changes or cancellations after 18:00 on the day before the stay, the hotel has the right to charge for the booking.
  9. Each guest receives an individual confirmation when the reservation is made.
